the supervised model are evaluated based on not just on their test scores but their results are always evaluated by comparing the results between training set and testing set.

The absolute metric alone does not gurantee that the model is accurate, but a relative coparision of metric such as RMSE between training and test set dictates how well the model has generalised and not memorised the data while training.
